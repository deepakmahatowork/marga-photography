This 15 day photography expedition offers an extraordinary journey through Nepal's most visually dramatic landscapes and cultural spectacles. Anchored by the world famous Tiji Festival in Lo Manthang the medieval walled capital of Upper Mustang you will photograph sacred masked dances (Chham) performed by Buddhist monks in vibrant brocade robes to drive away evil spirits. Traverse stark trans Himalayan desert canyons, red sandstone cliffs and ancient sky caves carved into sheer cliff faces.
