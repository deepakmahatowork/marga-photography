Nepal's ancient shamanic traditions (*Jhankri Vidya*) predate Buddhism and Hinduism by millennia, rooted in deep animist communion with mountain spirits, sacred rivers, and ancestral healing.

This flagship masterclass takes an elite group of strictly 5 visual storytellers into the secluded Kham Magar highlands of Western Nepal, providing respectful, unprecedented front-row documentation of living spiritual practices that exist nowhere else on Earth.
