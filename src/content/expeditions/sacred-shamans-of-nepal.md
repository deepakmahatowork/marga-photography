Embark on an extraordinary 12 day cultural and photographic odyssey into Rukum East one of Nepal's most remote and authentic mountain regions. Centerpiece to this journey is the celebrated Jhakri Mela (Shaman Festival) in Lukum Village where dozens of shamans assemble in feathered crowns bronze bells and sacred attire to perform rhythmic drumming spiritual invocations and ancient healing rituals preserved across generations by the Kham Magar community. Beyond the shamanic celebrations, travel through traditional stone roofed settlements like Taksera, walk across Nepal's famous Kusma Suspension Bridge and explore the high altitude plateau of Dhorpatan Hunting Reserve Nepal's only wildlife sanctuary for rare alpine species like blue sheep.
