The Bisket Jatra is a spectacular celebration marking the Nepalese New Year in Bhaktapur. This ancient festival opens with a fierce tug of war between the city's quarters to pull the colossal chariot of the god Bhairav. The excitement reaches its climax with the erection of the Linga, a sacred pole whose fall symbolises victory over demonic forces. Thimi the festival turns a vibrant orange during Sindur Jatra where an ecstatic crowd showers each other with vermilion powder. The ritual ends with the impressive tongue piercing in Bode, an act of extreme devotion to ensure community prosperity. Between mystical fervor and explosions of colors this festival is a visceral immersion into the soul and ancient traditions of the Newar people.
