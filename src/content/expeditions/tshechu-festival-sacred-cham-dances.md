This 14 day photography journey is dedicated to documenting the sacred Tshechu festival at Shechen Monastery and major Tibetan Buddhist sanctuaries across the Kathmandu Valley and Himalayan foothills. Tshechu is a celebration of spiritual transformation honoring Guru Rinpoche (Padmasambhava). Over 2 intensive days at the monastery, they photographed Buddhist monks performing ancient Cham dances in elaborate brocade robes and hand carved deity masks to the resonant sound of dungchen trumpets, cymbals and drums. Beyond the monastic courtyards capture the timeless devotions at Boudhanath and Swayambhunath stupas traditional Newar architecture in Bandipur and stunning Himalayan sunrise views over Annapurna ranges.
