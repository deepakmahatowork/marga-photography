High above the roaring river gorge in the shadow of Annapurna, the cliff face drops sheer into the abyss. Here, the Gurung honey hunters continue a sacred communion with the cliff spirit Rangkemi that has spanned millennia.

Before any hunter touches the ladder, shamans sacrifice a rooster and chant invocations to appease the spirits. Below the cliff, bundles of green leaves and wormwood are lit to produce thick billowing smoke, subduing the world’s largest honey bees, *Apis laboriosa*.

To photograph this from an adjacent suspension rope requires nerves of steel and absolute confidence in one's gear. As the smoke parted, the hunter sliced through the golden comb with a 25-foot bamboo pole, his weathered eyes calm and unblinking behind a crude mesh veil.
